Privacy Policy

Effective September 14, 2026 · Last updated September 21, 2026

CATCHIVE stores your collection and capture information on your device by default. We process the account and publication data described below only when you choose to sign in to and use Catchive World.

1. Controller and privacy officer

Sooda Studio (Representative: Seungwon Lee) is the controller. The privacy officer is Seungwon Lee. Send access, correction, deletion, restriction, withdrawal, and privacy requests to l8whale8l@gmail.com.

Business registration no. 324-06-03657

2. Data we process

When you choose Apple or Google sign-in, Supabase Auth processes the provider account identifier, email, basic profile, and authentication session. World publications contain the image you selected, its name, caption, and chosen publisher name. We process name-change, publication, collection-consent, heart, import, report, and blocking records. Historical comment records remain private until account deletion or required moderation retention ends. Your chosen publisher name is public above World posts and can change once every seven days. Emails, account IDs, and sign-in provider profiles are not displayed. Publications remain linked internally to an account for ownership, deletion, reporting, and blocking. Comments are unavailable.

3. Data kept on your device and image processing

We do not upload your entire collection, original capture time, or location records. Uploaded images are decoded and re-encoded to remove EXIF and GPS metadata. Information visibly included in an image or caption may still identify a person, so review your submission before publishing. Camera access is used to capture subjects and process them on device. Optional location access records where a Catchive was first captured.

4. Retention

Unpublished uploads become eligible for cleanup after about one hour. Pending submissions remain until withdrawal or moderation. Approved publications have no automatic expiry and remain until withdrawal, moderator removal, or account deletion. Expiry, withdrawal, rejection, or moderator removal queues image deletion, with retries after temporary failures. Account data, private publication history, lifetime-heart records, and safety records remain until account deletion unless law or an active dispute requires limited separate retention.

5. Processors and international processing

Supabase provides authentication and databases; Render hosts the API in Singapore; Cloudflare R2 and Workers store and deliver images. Encrypted transfers occur when you use World, and processing continues until contract termination, account deletion, or the applicable retention period. Distributed infrastructure may process data in Singapore and other countries where those providers operate. You may avoid this processing by not using World or by deleting your account.

6. Deletion and imported copies

You can delete your account in the app after signing in again. Apple authorization is also revoked for Apple accounts. Account deletion removes server account, publication, and comment records and schedules image deletion. Historical comments are no longer public; the comment feature is unavailable. Only posts with collection enabled can be imported and edited personally in the app. View-only posts have no in-app collection or export action; screenshots and external copying cannot be fully prevented. Copies another user lawfully imported while a publication was available generally remain on that user's device after expiry, withdrawal, or account deletion. When a moderator removes harmful content, the app removes matching imports on a subsequent online check. Removal from offline devices or external copies cannot be guaranteed.

7. Security

We use encrypted transport, private image storage, short-lived per-user image grants, server-side access controls, image re-encoding, bounded input and request limits, and a loopback-only administrator tool with CSRF protection and audit records.

8. Your rights and contact

Use the in-app account deletion feature or contact l8whale8l@gmail.com to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or withdrawal. We verify the requester and respond without undue delay, subject to applicable law.

9. Children

Catchive World is available only to people aged 14 or older. Children under 14 must not create a World account or submit content.

10. Effective date and changes

This policy is effective September 14, 2026. Material changes will be announced in the app or service before they take effect.
