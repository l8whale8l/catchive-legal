2-2. 이벤트 참여와 경품 수령

이벤트에 참여하면 행사 식별자, 참여 부문, 작품과 좋아요 집계 기록을 처리합니다. 공개 순위에는 작품 설명의 앞 네 글자와 좋아요 수가 표시되며, 발표 후 당첨자의 농장주 이름과 부문이 공개됩니다.

당첨자가 별도로 동의하여 수령 신청을 할 때만 경품 발송용 이메일과 동의·신청 시각을 수집합니다. 목적은 당첨 확인과 경품 발송입니다. 이메일은 다른 참가자나 공개 순위에 표시되지 않으며, 본인과 운영자만 조회할 수 있습니다. 이메일 수집에 동의하지 않거나 수령 신청을 하지 않을 수 있으나 이 경우 경품을 받을 수 없습니다. 발송 확인 즉시 또는 행사 종료 30일 후 중 먼저 도래하는 때 이메일을 삭제합니다. 계정 삭제 시에도 함께 삭제합니다. 운영자가 상품권을 구매한 뒤 신청 이메일로 직접 발송하며, 광고·마케팅 발송에는 사용하지 않습니다.

2-3. 공개 농장과 방문 상호작용

농장은 기본적으로 비공개입니다. 농장 설정에서 공개를 선택하면 농장 이름, 농장에 배치한 캐치브의 누끼 이미지·이름·크기·움직임 설정·농장 안 배치 좌표, 배경 장식 이미지를 계정과 연결해 서버에 저장하고 로그인한 다른 이용자에게 표시합니다. 보관함 전체나 원본 사진·촬영 위치를 전송하지 않습니다. 공개 이미지에는 사용자가 꾸민 장면에 직접 포함된 정보가 보일 수 있습니다.

방문자가 남긴 하트, 가상의 똥 위치, 발사 식별자·횟수·시각, 청소 결과, 농장 공개 상태, 소유자의 농장 접속 시각과 누적 오프라인 시간, 신고·차단 기록을 처리합니다. 좌표는 게임 화면 안의 위치이며 GPS나 실제 위치가 아닙니다. 이 정보는 농장 표시와 갱신, 중복 처리 방지, 방문자별 일일 사용량 제한, 접속 중 자연 생성 방지 및 안전 운영에 사용합니다. 쓰다듬기·사료 먹기·캐치브 움직임의 매 프레임 기록이나 녹화 영상을 서버로 전송하지 않습니다.

공개 농장은 방문자의 가상 똥 발사·청소와 하트를 허용합니다. 방문 도구는 자기 농장을 공개한 이용자만 사용할 수 있고, 하트는 자신의 농장 공개 여부와 관계없이 남길 수 있습니다. 설정에서 언제든 비공개로 바꿔 새 방문과 상호작용을 중단할 수 있습니다. 비공개 전환은 저장된 농장 자료나 이미 방문자의 기기에 표시된 이미지까지 삭제하는 기능은 아닙니다. 농장 사진의 앱 내 수집·내보내기는 제공하지 않지만 화면 캡처를 완전히 막을 수는 없습니다.

방문 알림을 허용하면 앱 설치 식별자, Apple 푸시 알림 토큰, 알림 환경과 계정 연결, 알림 설정, 갱신 시각을 저장합니다. 방문자가 똥을 발사하거나 청소한 경우 농장주에게 한국 날짜 기준 하루 최대 한 번 알림을 보내기 위해 농장·농장주·방문자 식별자, 행동 종류, 날짜와 발송 상태를 처리합니다. 토큰과 알림 설정은 다른 이용자에게 공개하지 않으며 광고 목적으로 사용하지 않습니다.

알림은 iOS 권한과 농장 설정의 방문 알림을 통해 선택하며 언제든 끌 수 있습니다. 앱에서 끄면 추가 발송을 중단하지만 이미 Apple에 전달된 알림이 도착할 수 있습니다. 로그아웃하면 해당 기기의 계정 연결 해제를 요청하며, 계정 삭제 시 기기 등록과 알림 기록도 삭제합니다.

2. Data we process

When you choose Apple or Google sign-in, Supabase Auth processes the provider account identifier, email, basic profile, and authentication session. World publications contain the image you selected, its name, caption, and chosen farm owner name. We process name-change, publication, collection-consent, heart, import, report, and blocking records. Historical comment records remain private until account deletion or required moderation retention ends. Your chosen farm owner name is public above World posts and can change once every seven days. Emails, account IDs, and sign-in provider profiles are not displayed. Publications remain linked internally to an account for ownership, deletion, reporting, and blocking. Comments are unavailable.

2-1. Sign-in provider SDK

Google Sign-In may process account/device identifiers, approximate location derived from IP, and usage information for authentication, fraud prevention and service improvement. CATCHIVE requests no additional phone-number or contact-list scopes. See Google’s SDK data notice and privacy policy.

2-2. Event entries and prize claims

For event entries we process the campaign, category, submitted work and likes. Public rankings show only the first four caption characters and the like count. After results are published, the winners’ farm owner names and categories are public.

Only a winner who separately consents and submits a claim provides a delivery email and consent/submission times. We use them to verify the claim and deliver the prize. The email is visible only to the winner and the operator, never to other participants or public rankings. You may refuse consent or decline to claim, but we then cannot deliver the prize. The email is erased upon delivery confirmation or 30 days after the event ends, whichever is earlier, and is also erased upon account deletion. The operator buys the voucher and emails it manually; claim emails are not used for advertising.

2-3. Public farms and visitor interactions

Farms are private by default. If you enable public sharing in farm settings, we store your farm name, placed Catchive cutout images and names, size and movement settings, in-farm coordinates, and decoration images linked to your account, and show them to other signed-in users. We do not upload your entire collection, original photos, or capture location. Your decorated scene may visibly contain information you included.

We process farm hearts, virtual waste positions, shot identifiers, counts and times, cleaning results, sharing status, owner presence times and accumulated offline time, and reports and blocks. Coordinates describe positions inside the game, not GPS or real-world location. These records support farm display and updates, deduplication, daily visitor limits, offline waste generation, and moderation. Frame-by-frame petting, feeding, and animal movement data, and farm recordings, are not sent to the server.

Public sharing allows other users to leave hearts and use virtual waste and cleaning tools. Visitor tools require the visitor to make their own farm public; hearts do not. You can make your farm private at any time to stop new visits and interactions. This does not delete the stored farm or images already displayed on a visitor’s device. Farm images have no in-app collection or export function, but screenshots cannot be completely prevented.

2-4. Optional farm activity notifications

If you allow notifications, we store an app installation identifier, Apple push token, notification environment and account association, preferences, and update time. To notify the owner of a visitor’s virtual waste or cleaning activity at most once per Korea calendar day, we process farm, owner and visitor identifiers, action type, date, and delivery status. Tokens and preferences are not public and are not used for advertising.

Notifications require iOS permission and can be disabled in farm settings. Disabling stops further delivery attempts; a notification already handed to Apple may still arrive. Signing out requests device deregistration. Account deletion also removes device registrations and notification records.

3. Data kept on your device and image processing

We do not upload your entire collection, original capture time, or location records. Uploaded images are decoded and re-encoded to remove EXIF and GPS metadata. Information visibly included in an image or caption may still identify a person, so review your submission before publishing. Camera access is used to capture subjects and process them on device. Optional location access records where a Catchive was first captured.

4. Retention

Unpublished uploads become eligible for cleanup after about one hour. Pending submissions remain until withdrawal or moderation. Approved publications have no automatic expiry and remain until withdrawal, moderator removal, or account deletion. Expiry, withdrawal, rejection, or moderator removal queues image deletion, with retries after temporary failures. Account data, private publication history, lifetime-heart records, and safety records remain until account deletion unless law or an active dispute requires limited separate retention.

Farm settings, images, hearts, and reports remain until replacement, removal, moderation, or account deletion. Replaced images enter the deletion queue. Virtual waste positions are removed when cleaned or the farm/account is deleted. Daily shot-limit and notification records older than seven Korea calendar days become eligible for cleanup during subsequent shot or notification cleanup work. Push registrations remain until sign-out deregistration, invalid-token removal, or account deletion; devices not refreshed for 60 days are excluded from delivery, and only the five most recently updated registrations per account are retained.

5. Processors and international processing

Supabase provides authentication and databases in Singapore; Render hosts the API in Singapore; Apple APNs delivers optional notifications using device push tokens and notification content. Cloudflare R2 and Workers store and deliver images. Encrypted transfers occur when you use World, Collect, public farms, or activity notifications, and processing continues until contract termination, account deletion, or the applicable retention period. Distributed infrastructure may process data in Singapore and other countries where those providers operate. You may avoid this processing by not using these online features and notifications, or by deleting your account.

6. Deletion and imported copies

You can delete your account in the app after signing in again. Apple authorization is also revoked for Apple accounts. Account deletion removes server account, publication, farm, heart, push registration, and comment records and schedules image deletion. Notification records for another farm remove the deleted visitor identifier. Virtual waste left in another farm contains no visitor account information and may remain until cleaned. Historical comments are no longer public; the comment feature is unavailable. Only posts with collection enabled can be imported and edited personally in the app. View-only posts have no in-app collection or export action; screenshots and external copying cannot be fully prevented. Copies another user lawfully imported while a publication was available generally remain on that user's device after expiry, withdrawal, or account deletion. When a moderator removes harmful content, the app removes matching imports on a subsequent online check. Removal from offline devices or external copies cannot be guaranteed.
